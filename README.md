# Portfolio

A simple website with a home page and a pricing page. It's plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so there's nothing to install.

## Pages
- `index.html`: home page
- `pricing.html`: pricing plans, a Monthly/Yearly switch, a cost calculator, FAQs and a "Get started" contact form

## How to change prices, company name or email
Open `assets/config.js` and edit the values. Everything else updates automatically.

## Contact form
- By default, clicking **Send request** opens the visitor's email app with their details filled in and addressed to `contactEmail`.
- To receive requests without the email app opening, create a free form at https://formspree.io and paste its link into `formEndpoint`.

## Viewing it
Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser. To put it online for free, turn on GitHub Pages under repo Settings → Pages → "Deploy from branch".
