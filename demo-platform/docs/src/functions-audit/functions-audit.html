Who to sign in as, what to show, and what the audit log records

Ten sign-ins in the order of the numbered sign-in list. Do the steps in each row and every function has been shown once. The last column is what each step writes to the audit log, so the closing audit check has something to show for every person.

10sign-ins · ~30 min
Before you start: open the site, choose Group of companies or One company on the first page. Same people, departments and approvals in both; the group adds company switching and Inter-company.
Signing in: pick the name in Who are you? and press OK — no password. Switch person with Switch role in the yellow banner. Finance, HR and executives in the group pick The whole group.

The audit function — show it last, as the System Administrator

  1. Administration › Audit log: every entry from steps 1–10 with when, who, module, action and what happened, newest first.
  2. Type a name or a number (Maya, TR-…, PO-…) in the search box above the table to follow one request end to end.
  3. Point out that it is append-only: there is no edit or delete, and financial and approval records are never deleted — only returned, declined or closed.
  4. Requests, approvals, postings, payroll and setting changes are all there, including the rate change and the email wording edit.
  5. Open any request (Requests › All requests): its History card shows the same trail for that one record, with its route.
  6. Reports › History tells the same story in plain words; choose Just me, My team or Everyone and an area such as Time off or Pay.

If time is short: skip rows 6 and 9 — nothing later depends on them (row 9 then has no pay run to approve). Keep row 10; the audit check is what ties the demo together.

Three things to repeat: no card numbers stored · the server checks every permission · nothing financial is ever deleted, and every action is in the audit log.
