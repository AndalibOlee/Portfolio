User manual: how to try the demo

About 30 minutes · works in any browser
1. Open the linkThe first page asks how your organization is set up (one company, or one or more companies) and which modules you want to see.
2. Pick your modulesHR, Requests, Team, Operations and Finance. Only what you pick appears. Home, Approvals, Email, Calendar, Reports and the audit log are always there.
3. Sign in as each personChoose a name under “Who are you?” and press OK. To switch person, press Switch role at the top. Follow the order below.

What to check in each module

Only the modules you picked are shown

HR

Maya, Liam, Priya, Rachel

  • Timesheets: type hours; overtime and banked time are worked out for you. The manager approves or sends back with a reason.
  • Time off: see the balance before sending; approved days fill the timesheet by themselves.
  • Payroll: pay runs and pay statements; staff see their own under My Pay.
  • Reviews, certificates, benefits and pension: expiry warnings 60 days ahead; each person sees their own plans.

Requests

Maya, Liam, Sofia, Grace

  • Five request types: travel request, credit card purchase, travel claim, expense claim and purchase order.
  • Attachments: receipts and quotes are added while filling in the form and stay with it.
  • Approval route: supervisor first; director level when over $5,000; then processed.
  • History: each request shows who did what and when. Nothing can be edited or removed.

Team

Maya, Liam

  • My Team: the manager’s page for the team’s people, time off, timesheets and requests.
  • Tasks: a manager assigns a task with steps and a due date; the employee updates progress with one click and adds notes.
  • Late or stuck tasks are flagged for the manager.

Operations

Ethan

  • Purchasing: purchase requests, purchase orders, receiving and matching to the bill.
  • Sales & Stock: sales orders, shipments and stock levels.
  • Projects and contracts: new projects go for approval before work starts.

Finance

Sofia, Daniel

  • Accounting: journal entries, month-end, budgets and the chart of accounts.
  • Bills, invoices and banking: approve and pay bills; match the bank feed.
  • Can be completely customized to your finance team’s needs.

Always included

Everyone

  • Approvals: one page with everything waiting on you, from the modules you picked.
  • Reports: on screen or downloaded for Excel; only reports for your modules.
  • Audit log (System Administrator): every action, with the person, the date and time, and what happened.
  • Email, Calendar and How-to guides with pictures of each screen.
Good to know. Everything you do is saved in your own browser only, so you can try anything. To start again with clean data, clear this site’s data in your browser. To change modules or the company setup, press Change on the sign-in page. All people, companies and figures are fictional.
