What to look at

Each module in a few lines. You only see the modules you picked.

HR

Timesheets, time off, payroll, reviews, certificates, benefits and pension

  • Type hours; overtime is worked out for you
  • See your time-off balance before you ask
  • Checklists for people joining and leaving
  • Warnings before a certificate expires

Requests

Travel, credit card purchases, travel and expense claims, purchase orders

  • One short form for each request
  • Attach receipts and quotes as you go
  • See who has it now and who is next
  • Every step is kept in its history

Team

For managers and their teams

  • Who is in, who is away
  • Give a task, follow its progress
  • Team timesheets and requests in one place

Operations

Purchasing, stock, projects and contracts

  • Purchase orders, receiving and matching
  • Sales orders and stock levels
  • New projects go for approval first

Finance

Shaped to your finance team’s needs

  • Accounting, month-end and budgets
  • Bills, invoices and banking

Always included

For everyone

  • Approvals: everything waiting for you
  • Reports: on screen or for Excel
  • Audit log: who did what, and when
  • How-to guides with pictures

Good to know

Try anything. What you do is saved only in your own browser.
