How to sign in and switch

  • Demo roles: on the sign-in page, under Or explore with a demo role, click the role.
  • Anyone else: pick the name in Who are you? and press OK — no password. The list is numbered in the presentation order (see the Presentation Sequence handout).
  • If asked, choose The whole group to see all four companies.
  • To change person: Switch role in the yellow bar at the top.

Before you start

  • Open the link in a private / incognito window so it starts from fresh demo data (data stays in that browser). On the shared link, reset it from Administration › Settings.
  • Screen at least 1360 px wide; on a projector, zoom to 90%.
  • Keep a small PDF handy for the receipt, the certificate, the contract and the travel confirmation uploads.
  • Run through Acts 1–2 once before presenting.

Good to know while presenting

  • Show the 3-way match on the existing bills (IDC-88812, SS-30417). Orders made from a request, such as PO-2026-0012, have no item lines, so there is nothing for the match to compare line by line. Don't link a supplier bill to it in Matching.
  • Don't approve a bill you entered yourself — the platform won't let you. Say it out loud as a control: nobody approves their own request or bill.
  • “End employment” is refused for the seven demo sign-ins, so they stay usable. Use Emma Lawson or another crew member.
  • Uploads are required in three places: the receipt on an expense claim, the new certificate on a renewal, and the booking confirmation before Finance marks a trip processed. Keep the small PDF ready.
  • Payroll as its own role: the Payroll Administrator button signs in Rachel Dubois, who can run the same pay run as Daniel.
  • The shared link needs no password. Anyone with it can pick any name; use the Administrator's Settings to reset.
  • Numbers like PO-2026-0012, EC-2026-0020, TR-2026-0020, PR-2026-0006 and CT-2026-0006 assume you start from fresh demo data. Dates assume a demo in the Nov 2 – Nov 15, 2026 pay period; on another day, use the current period and dates a few weeks ahead.
  • Lost? Esc closes any popup; Switch role in the yellow bar always returns to the sign-in page.

Talking points that land well

  • Built-in reporting line. Buttons name the approver (“Send to Liam”); requests climb by amount, never skipping a level.
  • Finance approves only its own team. Every other request goes to the requester's manager, then the Director above the amount; the administrator edits this in Approval rules.
  • Separation of duties. Nobody approves their own request or bill; Finance pays but the site receives.
  • Menus follow the role. Maya sees five menus, Daniel sees the whole finance side; every page also checks permission on its own.
  • Contracts follow the ladder. Liam, his managers and the executives see the grapple contract; HR does not.
  • Everything is recorded. The audit log and each record's history show who did what, and when.
