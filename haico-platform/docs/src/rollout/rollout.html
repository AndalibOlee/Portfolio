Who you need

  • Sponsor (CEO) — signs off checkpoints, clears roadblocks
  • Project lead (you) — plan, weekly status, go / no-go calls
  • Developers (1–2) — build, data loads, fixes
  • HR lead — staff records, leave, benefits, pension
  • Payroll lead — rates, deductions, side-by-side runs
  • Finance lead — chart of accounts, vendors, opening balances
  • IT / security — Microsoft sign-in, hosting, backups
  • Champion per company — Alderbrook, Ridgeline, Silver Lake, Northbay

Biggest risks — and the fix

  • Wrong pay. Use CRA-certified payroll formulas; go live only after 2 pay runs match the old system to the cent.
  • Messy data. Two trial loads; totals must match the old books before go-live.
  • Privacy. Hosting in Canada, MFA, role-based access, audit log (BC PIPA).
  • Busy season. Silver Lake Lodges and Northbay Seafoods go live in March, before their season.
  • Mid-year payroll switch. Carry year-to-date totals across so T4s are right.
  • People don't adopt it. Champions, 1-hour role training, in-app how-to guides.
