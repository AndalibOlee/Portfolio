DEMO Enterprise Platform · Group of companies

Presentation sequence — one story across ten sign-ins

Sign in as these people in this order. Each sign-in shows the functions that person owns, and the story carries from one to the next, so by step 10 every function has been shown once. The sign-in list is numbered in this order.

10sign-ins
On the first page choose Group of companies. Finance, HR and the executives then pick The whole group (or one company) after signing in.
The story: Maya plans a trip, buys a life jacket, claims a coffee run and books a day off. Liam decides. Sofia and Daniel process and pay. Priya hires, Rachel runs payroll, Ethan gets it when Liam is away, Grace and Olivia decide the big ones, and the administrator shows the record of it all.
Sign in asFunctions shownDo this (in order)
1Maya CollinsEmployeeHome · Requests (travel, card, travel claim, expense claim, PO) · Time off · My Time with banked OT · My Tasks · My Pay · My Documents
  1. Home: the summary tiles and Needs your attention.
  2. New Travel Request ($1,200) and New Credit Card Purchase — no card number, no price; note “Goes to Liam”.
  3. New Purchase Order over $5,000 — director level will be needed.
  4. Time off: request two days; the balance check before sending.
  5. My Time: type a 10-hour day → overtime goes to Banked OT at 1.5×, live. Submit.
2Liam FosterManagerApprovals (approve, return with a comment, decline) · My Team · timesheet approval · Request access · Purchasing · Projects
  1. Approvals: approve the travel; return the card purchase (“add the link”); approve the PO — it moves to director level.
  2. Approve Maya's time off and her timesheet; her banked OT is confirmed.
  3. My Team › Request access: let Maya raise requests for a colleague.
  4. Email: the Outlook-style Inbox shows the messages this produced.
3Sofia MarquezAccountantFinance queue (process with GST/PST, pay a claim) · Accounts Payable (new bill needs account + department) · Accounts Receivable + Customers · 3-way match · Finance › PO · Rates & coding (needs approval) · Banking · Reports
  1. Finance queue: process Maya's travel — $1,000 + GST + PST, attach the confirmation; the ledger entry posts.
  2. Record an expense claim as paid.
  3. New bill: the account and department are required.
  4. Customers: add one. Matching: a bill with two POs.
  5. Finance › PO: where each PO is; open one, view only.
  6. Rates: change mileage → it waits for Daniel.
4Daniel OkaforFinance ManagerApprovals (rate change, bills) · Month-end · Inter-company · Payroll pages · At a glance on Home
  1. Home: At a glance at the top.
  2. Approvals: approve Sofia's rate change — Rates & coding shows the history.
  3. Payroll › Pay runs: open a run; banked overtime is not paid twice.
  4. Month-end checklist.
5Priya RamanHR ManagerHiring requests · Onboarding · Employees · Time & Attendance (everyone's time off and timesheets) · Stat holidays · Certificates
  1. Home: hiring requests in Needs your attention.
  2. Hiring requests: approve one; it becomes a job posting.
  3. Payroll › Stat holidays: add one — timesheets pick it up.
  4. Time & Attendance › Time off: everyone's balances.
6Rachel DuboisPayroll AdministratorTimesheets by pay period · hours report · pay statements · CRA remittances · year-end (T4, ROE)
  1. By pay period: who has submitted; the report unlocks when all are approved.
  2. Year-end: a T4 preview.
7Ethan ClarkeCompany AdministratorThe away rule · All requests (own company only) · Receiving
  1. Approve a full day off for Liam (today).
  2. Switch to Maya, submit anything: it goes to Liam or Ethan. Back as Ethan: it's in Approvals; approve it — gone from Liam's list.
  3. Receiving: receive a purchase order.
8Grace HollowayExecutive DirectorDirector level · All requests (read only) · executive Home
  1. Approvals: Maya's PO over $5,000 — approve; the purchase order prints on one page.
  2. All requests: everything, read only.
9Olivia BennettCEOSame pages as Grace · director level when Grace is away
  1. Approvals: decide Chloe Nguyen's $6,200 trip and Sofia's $7,800 purchase order, both waiting at director level.
  2. Home: Company at a glance for the group.
10System AdministratorSuper adminUsers & access · Approval rules · Audit log · Email templates · Companies · Page & Experience
  1. Audit log: every step from 1–9, who and when.
  2. Approval rules: the $5,000 director limit.
  3. Email templates: edit one line of wording.

Timing: about 3 minutes a sign-in; 30 minutes for the whole story. Steps 6 and 9 can be skipped if time is short — nothing later depends on them. In the group, also show the company switcher in the top bar and Finance › Accounting › Inter-company.

If something is asked that isn't in the demo: “Good question — I'll write it on the page and it goes into the next round.” Press Request a change on that page. To switch setups, sign out and press Change on the sign-in page.
