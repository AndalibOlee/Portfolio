      How an engagement runs

      From "our systems don't talk to each other" to a back office that runs itself.

      A staged approach. Each phase ends with a deliverable your leadership reviews and approves before we move on.

      01

      Discover

      Conversations with staff, finance and leadership. We learn what hurts and why.

      → Findings brief
      02

      Map

      Document current processes, data and reporting obligations end to end.

      → Process maps & requirements
      03

      Decide

      Compare buy, build or improve, looking at total cost of ownership, not just sticker price.

      → Options analysis & roadmap
      04

      Deliver

      Build or implement, migrate, test and train, with vendors managed against the plan.

      → Live system, trained team
      05

      Sustain

      Ongoing bookkeeping, support and improvements so the gains stick.

      → Monthly service reports
