Frequently asked questions

What is the one-time setup fee for?

It covers installing and configuring the platform for your business, importing your data and training your team. You pay it once at the start.

What counts as an "active user"?

Anyone who logs in at least once during the month. Employees who don't use the platform that month aren't billed.

How does the introductory offer work?

For your first months, the per-user fee is % off. After that, the regular price applies. The setup fee isn't discounted.

What's the difference between monthly and yearly billing?

Monthly billing is flexible and billed every month. Yearly billing is paid once a year and costs % less.

What if my company grows?

If you move into a larger size group, we'll move you to the matching plan. Just let us know.
